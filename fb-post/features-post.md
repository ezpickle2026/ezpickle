🥒 Run your courts. We'll handle the rest. 🏓

EzPickle is the all-in-one system for pickleball facilities in the Philippines 🇵🇭

🎥 PICKLE CAM
Your players get replays of their own games, ready to watch and share after their slot. They can watch their court live while they play, too.

📱 QR CHECK-IN
Every booking comes with a QR code. Staff scan it with a phone or a handheld scanner, and the player is on court in seconds.

✅ ONLINE BOOKINGS
Players see open slots live, book in under a minute and pay with GCash, Maya, GrabPay, QR Ph or card. Double-booking can't happen.

📅 COURT CALENDAR
Every court in day, week and month views. Walk-ins, reschedules and cancellations take a couple of taps.

👥 CUSTOMERS VIEW
Everyone who has booked or played with you in one list, with their bookings, Open Play games and total spend.
