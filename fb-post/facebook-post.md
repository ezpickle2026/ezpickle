## Main post (copy-paste)

🥒 Play Easy. Play More. Play EzPickle. 🏓

Booking a pickleball court shouldn't take 20 Messenger replies. That's why we built EzPickle: a complete court booking and facility system made for pickleball in the Philippines 🇵🇭

✅ LIVE COURT AVAILABILITY
See open slots on every court as they happen. No more "available pa po ba?" 😅

⚡ BOOK IN UNDER 60 SECONDS
Pick a date, court and time, then pay with GCash, Maya, GrabPay, QR Ph or card. Your slot is held while you pay, and double-booking can't happen.

📱 QR CHECK-IN
Every booking comes with a QR code. Show it at the front desk and you're on court.

👥 OPEN PLAY THAT SORTS ITSELF OUT
Come alone, leave with a crew. Join as a single player and our auto-stacker builds skill-balanced groups across the courts, with a waitlist when the session is full.

📊 FOR COURT OWNERS
• Live dashboard: today's revenue, bookings and court utilization
• Day, week and month calendar
• Walk-ins, rescheduling, cancellations and refunds
• Inventory and counter sales (paddles, balls, drinks, gear)
• Sales and utilization reports with CSV export
• Promo codes, staff roles and an audit log

Running a pickleball facility? Let's talk. Message us to see EzPickle in action 👇
