What your players get

BookingPayments

Live availability and a 5-step booking flow

Players pick a date, court and time on a grid that updates in real time as others book. Their slot is held for 15 minutes while they pay with GCash, Maya, GrabPay, QR Ph or card through PayMongo's secure checkout. Peak and off-peak rates are priced automatically, even across a booking that spans both.

MobileQR check-in

Made for phones, with QR check-in

Most bookings happen on a phone, so every screen is designed mobile-first. No app to download.

  • Booking confirmation by email with a signed QR code.
  • Front desk scans it with a phone camera or handheld scanner.
  • Customer dashboard with upcoming bookings, payment history and profile.
  • Promo codes for launches, loyal players and partners.

Feature highlights · Open Play
Feature highlights

Open Play that sorts itself out

Open Play

Join solo, with a waitlist when it's full

Players browse sessions by skill level, join and pay as individuals, and are added to a waitlist automatically when the session fills. When someone drops out, the next player is promoted.

Staff tool

One-click, skill-balanced court stacking

The auto-stacker groups players by rating and avoids repeating the same pairings across rounds. Courts never go over capacity, leftovers go to a clearly marked bench, and staff can move anyone by hand afterwards.
